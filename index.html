How to Use the Simulator

1. Standard Practice: By default, you'll receive random prompts for Task 1 and Task 2. Type your essay in the right panel. The word counter updates live.

2. Custom Mode: Click the "Custom Mode" button (top right) to paste your own prompts or even your own Band 9 model answers for practice.

3. Band 9 Model Answers: If a Band 9 answer is available for the current prompt, use the "Below" or "Side" buttons to reveal it and study the structure.

4. Timer: Click on the time (e.g., 60:00) to adjust the starting time. The timer turns red when less than 5 minutes remain.

5. Font Size: Use the A- and A+ buttons to resize the text to your preference.
